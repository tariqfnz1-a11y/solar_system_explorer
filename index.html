📖 Key Space Concepts & Grade 6 Vocabulary

Master these 6 core astronomy ideas using everyday comparisons!

6 Essential Terms
🔄 1 Year

Orbit & Revolution

An orbit is the curved path a planet follows around the Sun. One complete trip around the Sun is called a revolution, which equals 1 year on that planet!

Everyday Analogy: Like running one full lap around a circular racetrack!
🌐 1 Day & Night

Rotation & Axis

Rotation is a planet spinning in place around its imaginary center line called an axis. One full spin creates day and night (24 hours on Earth).

Everyday Analogy: Like a basketball spinning on the tip of your finger!
🧲 Cosmic Glue

Gravity

Gravity is the invisible pulling force between objects with mass. Because the Sun is gigantic, its strong gravity pulls planets inward while their forward speed keeps them from crashing into it!

Everyday Analogy: Like swinging a ball on a string—the string (gravity) stops the ball from flying away!
📏 Space Ruler

Astronomical Unit (AU)

Space is so huge that miles and kilometers get too big to read easily! Scientists use the Astronomical Unit (AU), where 1 AU equals the average distance from Earth to the Sun (~150 million km).

Quick Example: Jupiter is 5.2 AU away—over 5 times farther from the Sun than Earth!
🪨 Two Planet Groups

Terrestrial vs. Giant Planets

The 4 inner planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are small, rocky Terrestrial worlds. Beyond the Asteroid Belt live the 4 huge outer worlds: Gas Giants (Jupiter, Saturn) and Ice Giants (Uranus, Neptune).

Everyday Analogy: Four tiny marbles near a campfire, and four giant beach balls way out in the yard!
☄️ Smaller Neighbors

Dwarf Planets, Asteroids & Comets

Asteroids are rocky leftovers mostly orbiting in the belt between Mars and Jupiter. Comets are icy "dirty snowballs" that grow glowing tails near the Sun. Dwarf planets (like Pluto) are round worlds that share their orbital neighborhood.

Remember: Pluto orbits in the icy Kuiper Belt past Neptune!
